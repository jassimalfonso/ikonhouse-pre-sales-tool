How to use

Five steps from a floor plan to a client-ready package. You can jump around — nothing here is locked.

  1. Add your floor plan. Drop a PDF or image onto the canvas, or use Floors → Add floor. Multi-page PDFs become one floor per page. Then Crop to trim it to the drawing itself — with several plans, Apply to all plans gives them matching framing so the pages line up when flipping through.
  2. Set up your device library. Create a category, then add devices to it with a name, colour and icon. Prices are optional — add them and the BoQ totals itself. The library is saved inside each project.
  3. Place your ikons. Tap a device, then tap the plan. Keep tapping to place more. Drag to reposition — they snap into line with each other so runs look tidy.
  4. Draw rooms. Use Skribble to brush over an area — release and it becomes a room — or turn on Rooms to drag a box or tap corner-to-corner. Rooms decide which devices belong where and can be marked out of scope. Use Room schedule order in the toolbar to arrange them — that order becomes the row order in the FD sheet and BoQ.
  5. Export. Choose a paper size and take the PDF plan set, the Excel FD sheet and BoQ, or the full package — which includes the editable design file so anyone can reopen it.
Save as you go — Ctrl+S writes straight back to your project file. Made a mistake? Ctrl+Z undoes almost everything, including room edits.

Everything responds to the same few gestures. Tap to select, double-tap to edit, hold to create.

Making it yours

The palette button in the toolbar — also on the welcome screen — opens Appearance, where you can pick a colour theme: Bronze, Indigo, Green, Azure, Ember, or Mono for pure black and white. Each works in day or night, is remembered for every project you open, and Back to the original restores Bronze in one click.

Shortcuts

R rooms · K skribble · A notes · O out of scope · C crop · G greyscale · N numbering · L library · ? this guide · pressing the same key again, Esc, or the Done button leaves a tool

Getting around

Drag the plan to move it · Shift-drag to select several ikons · double-click the scroll wheel to fit the plan to the window · pinch or Ctrl+scroll to zoom · swipe, two fingers, or Space+drag to pan · tap outside the plan to cancel whatever you are doing

Ikons

Tap a device then tap the plan to place · drag to move — they snap into rows and columns · drag a box on empty plan to select several (on touch, hold an ikon then tap others) · Shift-click to add one to the selection · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V to copy a cluster · Delete to remove

Rooms & walls

Skribble — brush over an area and release, and it becomes a room, ready to fine-tune · skribbled rooms come out with square walls (adjust the corners afterwards for an odd shape) · the brush stops at existing rooms and the new room snaps flush against their walls, so rooms fit together without gaps (Alt to ignore) · drag for a quick box · tap corner to corner for any shape, then tap the first corner to close · drag a wall to move it — straight by default, Shift frees it · drag a corner to reshape, Shift squares it, Alt turns snapping off · where two rooms share a corner both move together — hold Ctrl to move just one, or switch off Joined rooms move together · double-tap a wall to split it · hold a corner (or Ctrl-drag) to grow a new room that stays joined · double-tap a corner to remove it · tap a wall to highlight its room, tap again to step to the room on the other side · mark a room as an outdoor area to have it surround other rooms instead of dividing them

Notes

Press A or tap Notes, then tap the plan to leave a comment — site conditions, client requests, anything worth remembering. Each note is a target ring on the thing it describes, joined by a leader line to a label — drag either end, so one note can call out a single device, several items at one point, or a whole area. Tap a note to open it — the editor takes as many lines as you need. Clearing the text deletes it. Notes print with their leaders on the exported sheets.

Layers

Each device and each category has an eye in the library — switch one off to take it off the plan and off the exported sheets, for showing a client just the lighting, or just the audio. Quantities in the BoQ are unaffected: hiding is about presentation, not scope.

Library

Drag the ⠿ grip to dock it left, right, top, bottom or floating · ▲▼ or hold to reorder categories · ▲▼ on a device reorders it in its category · drag a device onto another to drop it into that spot, or onto a category header to move it there

Everything you export carries your branding, the project details, and the date.

Plan sheets (PDF / PNG)

A cover page plus one sheet per floor, at A4, A3 or A1. Each sheet carries the plan and a legend grouped by category. Turn on auto-numbering and every ikon prints a code such as CS-01. Ikon size and opacity carry through from the toolbar sliders, as does greyscale if you turn it on.

Excel

An FD sheet listing rooms down the side and devices across the top, in your library's order, with out-of-scope rooms shaded. Plus a priced BoQ Summary.

Full package

A zip with the PDF, the Excel, the sheet images, and the editable .ikonplan file — with a note explaining how to reopen it. Send this and the recipient has everything.

Checking as you go

Open the BoQ drawer any time for live totals and a room-by-room matrix, so the numbers are never a surprise at export time.
